Our Culinary Mission

From A Small Local Kitchen To The City's Most Loved Food Fleet

In 2018, Stackly was founded on a simple realization: takeout shouldn't mean soggy crusts, lukewarm curries, or hour-long waits. Food is an experience, best enjoyed sizzling from the grill or chilled fresh from the sushi bar.

We built a revolutionary logistics network that links top-rated artisan kitchens, master bakers, and neighborhood gems directly with hyper-local delivery couriers equipped with custom thermal-sealed packs.

Today, we proudly deliver over a million smiles a year - from woodfired pizzas and smash burgers to fresh poke bowls and organic baked pastries.
