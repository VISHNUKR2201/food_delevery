Instant Answers

Frequently Asked Support Questions

Clear, transparent answers to our diners', partner restaurants', and riders' most common questions.

How fast will customer support respond to my order issue?

Live chat and emergency phone hotline inquiries are answered by our Salem support desk in under 90 seconds on average. If you submit a ticket via our inquiry form, our customer happiness team will respond with a full solution within 15 minutes.

Contact Support About This

What happens if my food arrives cold, late, or damaged?

We back every delivery with our 24-Minute Freshness Guarantee. If your order exceeds our promised time window, arrives with broken tamper seals, or isn't steaming hot, simply tap 'Report Issue' or contact our hotline. We immediately issue an instant kitchen redelivery or a 100% refund voucher to your wallet.

Contact Support About This

Can I change my delivery address or phone number after ordering?

Yes, as long as the courier has not already departed the partner kitchen. Call our emergency hotline immediately or use our live chat tool with your Order ID, and our dispatch desk will reroute your courier in real-time at no extra charge.

Contact Support About This

How do restaurants and cloud kitchens partner with Stackly?

Select 'Restaurant Kitchen Partnership' in our contact form or email partners@stacklyfood.com. Our culinary onboarding specialist will verify your FSSAI license, set up our kitchen order management tablet, photograph your signature dishes, and have your menu live within 48 hours with 0% commission for the first 30 days.

Contact Support About This

What are the requirements to join Stackly as an express courier?

You need a valid two-wheeler driving license, Aadhaar verification, and a smartphone with GPS. If you do not own a two-wheeler, Stackly offers subsidized electric scooter rentals with unlimited battery swaps across our Salem depot network.

Contact Support About This

Do you manage large corporate catering, office lunches, and parties?

Yes! Our corporate catering desk coordinates group orders for 20 to 1,000+ attendees with customized dietary labels (Jain, Keto, Vegan, Halal), individually sealed thermal boxes, and scheduled batch courier drops with corporate billing invoices.

Contact Support About This
